India’s coffee story spans diverse elevations, climates and growing traditions. The right origin depends on the profile, availability and specification your market requires.

A diverse growing country

Different landscapes. Different possibilities.

Indian coffee is associated with southern growing regions such as Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Well-known coffee belts include Coorg, Chikmagalur, Hassan and Wayanad.

These are regional references, not a claim that every region or lot is currently available through Organic Rays. Origin and traceability details are confirmed for each offered lot.
