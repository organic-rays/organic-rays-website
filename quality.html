Quality & Sourcing

Clarity before commitment.

Quality is not a slogan. It is a set of verifiable details attached to a specific coffee lot, supplier and shipment.

A practical, lot-specific approach

For each qualified enquiry, the relevant product and supply details should be established before a commercial offer is made. This includes origin, variety, processing, grade, crop year, packaging and any requested quality documentation.

What buyers can discuss with us

  • Product type, variety and target cup or physical profile
  • Origin and traceability information available for the offered lot
  • Moisture, screen size, defect and grading parameters, where applicable
  • Processing, bag type, packing weight and shipment configuration
  • Sampling, inspection and documentation requirements
  • Destination market, delivery terms and shipment planning

Documentation and certifications

Any certificates, test reports, audit documents or regulatory declarations will be shared only when valid, current and applicable to the specific supplier, facility or lot. Organic Rays does not make blanket certification claims on this website.

Buyers should include mandatory standards and destination-country requirements in their enquiry so these can be checked before any offer is issued.

Have a buyer specification sheet?

Send it with your enquiry so the requirements can be reviewed accurately.
