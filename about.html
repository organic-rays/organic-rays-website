About Organic Rays

Indian Coffee. Global Taste.

We are building an export-focused Indian coffee brand with a simple ambition: make it easier for international buyers to explore coffee from India through clear communication and dependable trade relationships.

Our purpose

Bring India’s coffee story to more markets.

Organic Rays aims to connect the diversity of Indian coffee with importers, distributors, roasters and businesses around the world.

Our approach is grounded in listening to buyer requirements, understanding sourcing realities and communicating product details honestly.

What guides us

Principles for long-term trade.

Transparency

Clear, lot-specific information and no unsupported quality or certification claims.

Responsiveness

Practical communication around specifications, samples, timelines and buyer needs.

Partnership

A long-term outlook with buyers and supply partners, built through trust and follow-through.
